## Use of Generative AI

Contributions ***must not*** include content generated by LLMs (Claude, Copilot, ChatGPT, Gemini, and so on). This includes
any code, documentation, pull requests, and issues.

For bug reports, you are allowed to submit LLM-assisted findings (i.e. POCs), since we have no way to stop you. However,
your report should be human-written and human-understood such that you can explain it to any maintainers.
